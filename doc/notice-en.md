# The T-Project

A small planet lost in an anonymous dimension, Gaia is unique in that it bears within its core an incredible flow of energy used by its inhabitants in the form of magic. This provoked numerous conflicts between humans and the wildlife populating Gaia. From this omnipresent thirst for power was born a mysterious organization, the FFC°, which quickly managed to crush its opponents and rule over all of Gaia. Only its highest leaders know that it was created to realize an insane project: the conquest of Power, the key to which is buried kilometers underground in the form of a sleeping demon…

- Gaia Diameter: 5480 Km
- Satellites: Eryan, Soltar
- Average Temperature: 28°C
- Currency: the Skull

## World Map

<img src="./images/05-world-1.bmp" width="256" style="image-rendering: pixelated;" />

Move around the map using the directional keys. You can press `[MENU]` at any time to check your items or stats.

There is a heliport on each continent. They allow you to travel very quickly, without being attacked, and have the significant advantage of being free.

## Towns

Towns accessible to you are represented by a green square on the map; they are listed on the left. Advancing in the scenario will allow you to discover new towns.

Move around towns using the directional keys. You can meet characters in houses or, sometimes, in the streets.

<img src="./images/02-dialog-botma.bmp" width="256" style="image-rendering: pixelated;" />

Press `[MENU]` to check your stats or use an item.

<img src="./images/06-menu.bmp" width="256" style="image-rendering: pixelated;" />

Press `[F6]` to leave the town and return to the world map.

Towns are decorated with scenery elements: trees, rocks, water, etc.

A ![P](./images/inn.bmp) indicates the presence of an inn, where you can save the game. They also allow you to rest for the modest sum of 25 skulls, which will restore your stats to their maximum.

<img src="./images/08-auberge-1.bmp" width="256" style="image-rendering: pixelated;" />

<img src="./images/08-auberge-2.bmp" width="256" style="image-rendering: pixelated;" />

A ![°](./images/shop.bmp) indicates the presence of a shop. You will find them in towns, but also in the world from time to time, in the form of traveling merchants. Buy all the items you need there; prices will vary depending on the shop. Warning: some items are not for sale in shops, such as groseyes for example.

### Learn
Use this action whenever the enemy uses an attack or spell that you haven't mastered yet. This allows you to instantly learn that move, and reuse it during combat. You can only memorize 5 attaks and 5 spells: you will quickly need to delete some to learn new ones.

### Fleeing
Dying with sword in hand is still dying. Fleeing is no dishonor.

<img src="./images/99-game-over.bmp" width="256" style="image-rendering: pixelated;" />

### Combat Mechanics

- Z's level increases with the number of victories.
- Leveling up increases stats (att, def, life, force, magic).

### End of Combat
If you emerge victorious from the fight, you will win:

- Experience points, which allow you to increase your fighter levels.
- A reward for every monster killed on Gaia.
- Various items.
- Progressive improvements to your statistics.

## Tips
- Explore the cities thoroughly to progress in the game;
- In combat, it can be useful to reduce the opponent's speed or defense. It becomes easier to defeat them;
- At the start of the game, it can be useful to level up by fighting a bit before going to TARA;
- Upon arriving in a new city, stop by the inn to rest and save;
